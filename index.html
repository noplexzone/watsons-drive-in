FAQ & rules

Clear expectations before guests pull in.

Can I buy tickets now?

No. Tickets Coming Soon. The first live version is planned to support built-in purchasing and QR check-in.

Can I order food from my car?

Mobile ordering is planned. The first static website previews the menu without accepting real orders.

Is outside food allowed?

Outside food and drinks may be limited. Guests are encouraged to support the concession stand.

Will alcohol be allowed?

Outside alcohol is not permitted. Event-specific policies may be posted when applicable.

What happens when weather turns bad?

Weather updates will be posted before showtime when needed. Severe weather may delay or cancel shows.
